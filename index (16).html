Our Story

About Duro's Crafts

Every stitch tells a story — here's mine.

HELLO,

Meet Duro

My name is Duro Hellen Mulaku,the founder,the brain and the designer behind Duro’s Crafts.

My journey with crochet began with a love for creating beautiful things by hand. Over time, that love grew into something bigger,a brand where creativity meets craftsmanship, and ordinary yarn is transformed into something extraordinary.

I’m passionate about creating original designs, exploring new inspirations and colour combinations,and making pieces that allow people to express their individual style.

Beyond fashion,Duro’s Crafts is also a home for fellow creatives, offering yarns, crochet hooks, darning needles, stitch markers, and other craft supplies to inspire them to bring their own ideas to life.

For me,crochet is more than a craft. It’s an art, a form of expression, and a reminder that beautiful things take time.

Welcome to Duro’s Crafts,a slow fashion brand built on creativity,passion and the beauty of handmade artistry.

Our Policies
🔄 Return & Alteration Policy
▼

At Duro's Crafts, customer satisfaction is important to us. Please review our policy before placing an order.

  • Requests for alterations or returns must be made within 48 hours of receiving your order.
  • Items must be unworn, unused, and not damaged by the client.
  • Alterations or replacements will only be accepted if the error was on our side. If incorrect measurements were provided by the client, the request will not qualify for a free alteration or replacement.
  • Shipping costs for returned items are the responsibility of the client. If the error was due to incorrect measurements or any other error on the client's side, additional alteration charges may apply before the item is corrected and returned. If the error was on our side, we will cater for shipping and alteration charges.

We do not offer refunds. We will either:

  1. Make a replacement item, or
  2. Rectify the returned item and send it back to you.

By placing an order with Duro's Crafts, you agree to the terms of this policy.

📦 Shipping Policy
▼
  • All items are handmade to order. Production times vary and will be communicated before ordering.
  • Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information. We are not liable for delays caused by incorrect addresses.
  • Delivery fee is charged according to location and will be communicated before dispatch.
  • The shipping fee must be paid before dispatch. Your order will not be sent out until this payment is received.
  • Delivery times are estimates and may vary due to courier services, weather, or other factors beyond our control.
  • You will receive shipping confirmation and tracking information once your order is dispatched.
  • Duro's Crafts is not responsible for parcels lost, delayed, or damaged in transit — claims should be directed to the courier. We will help track your item.
  • Orders cannot be cancelled once production has begun or after they have been shipped.
