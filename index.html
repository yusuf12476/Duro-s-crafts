Hello, I'm Duro

The Hands & Heart Behind Every Piece

What started as a quiet hobby during long evenings has grown into a little world of handmade love. I remember my first crochet hook — a simple plastic one — and the very first chain stitch I ever made. It was crooked, uneven, and absolutely perfect to me.

Every piece I create carries intention, care, and creativity. I believe that handmade items carry an energy that mass-produced things simply cannot — you can feel the love in every loop, every stitch, every knot.

Whether it's a blanket for a new baby, a custom gift for someone special, or a pattern for you to make yourself — I want every creation from this little shop to bring warmth, joy, and a touch of handmade magic into your life.

The Duro's Crafts Values

Everything I make is guided by a few simple beliefs about what handmade should feel like.

💛
Made with Intention
Every stitch is placed thoughtfully. No rushing, no shortcuts — just careful, loving craftsmanship from start to finish.

Quality Materials

✨
Truly Personalised

Community First
